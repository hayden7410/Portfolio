Design a modern, professional portfolio website for a Business Systems Analyst / Business Analyst with technical implementation experience.

The portfolio should feel like a polished enterprise SaaS/product website rather than a generic developer portfolio. The design should communicate structured thinking, business analysis, systems analysis, process improvement, requirements management, system design, and technical delivery.

STYLE
- Clean, modern, professional, minimal
- Light theme with white cards and a very light gray/blue background
- Primary accent color: professional blue
- Dark navy text
- Thin subtle borders
- Soft shadows only where needed
- Rounded corners, but not overly playful
- Plenty of whitespace
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Use a modern sans-serif font such as Inter
- Avoid excessive gradients, neon colors, glassmorphism, or flashy developer aesthetics
- The website should feel suitable for recruiters, hiring managers, Business Analysts, Product Managers, and technology teams
- Visually similar to a modern enterprise SaaS dashboard or consulting portfolio

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
- Fully responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Recruiter-friendly
- Easy to scan within 30–60 seconds
- Project case studies should be the strongest part of the website
- Avoid huge paragraphs
- Prefer short explanations, diagrams, metrics, screenshots, and artifact previews
- Reusable project components so future projects can be added easily
- Use subtle microinteractions for cards and buttons
- Keep animations professional and minimal
- Accessibility-friendly contrast and readable font sizes
